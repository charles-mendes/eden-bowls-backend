This Policy explains what personal information Eden Bowls collects when you use edenbowls.com and our fresh dog food subscription, why we collect it, who we share it with and what choices you have. It is written to be read, not just accepted. If anything is unclear, write to privacy@edenbowls.com.

## 1. Who we are

The website and the subscription service are operated by EZER ONE INC, doing business as Eden Bowls, based in Boca Raton, Florida (“Eden Bowls”, “we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal information described in this Policy.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you create an account and build a plan, you provide your name, email address, phone number, password, and shipping and billing addresses. About your dog, you provide name, breed, age, weight, activity level, dietary restrictions and recipe preferences. Dog information is used only to calculate portions and build the plan.

**Payment information.** Payments are processed by Stripe. Your full card number never touches our servers. We receive from Stripe only the last four digits, card brand, expiration date and the confirmation of each charge.

**Information collected automatically.** IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, date and time of access, and cookie identifiers. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, in aggregate.

**Communications.** Emails you exchange with us, requests to our customer care team (including photos you send to report a problem with an order), and the record of automated emails we send (subscription confirmation, shipping, billing, pause and cancellation).

We do not collect sensitive personal information about you, such as health data, precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin or biometric data. Information about your dog is not sensitive personal information, but we treat it with the same care.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- create and maintain your account and your dog’s plan;
- prepare, ship and deliver meals;
- charge your subscription and issue receipts and invoices;
- send transactional emails about your subscription (confirmation, shipping, billing, price change notices, pause, resume, cancellation and password reset);
- respond to your requests and review problems with orders;
- send news and offers, if you opt in (you can opt out at any time through the link in every email);
- understand how the site is used and improve the service;
- detect and prevent fraud and protect account security;
- comply with tax, accounting and legal obligations.

We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Your dog’s suggested portion is calculated from the information you enter, and you can adjust the number of packs on the My plan page or ask our team to review it at any time.

## 4. How we share information

We share personal information only with parties that need it to make the service work:

- **Stripe**, for payment processing and recurring billing.
- **UPS**, which ships our orders nationwide and receives your name, address and phone number for delivery.
- **Email delivery provider** and **hosting providers** for the website and database.
- **Google Analytics**, for aggregate site statistics.
- **Accounting and tax service providers**, to meet our legal obligations.
- **Public authorities**, when required by law, subpoena or court order.
- **A successor business**, if Eden Bowls is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this Policy will continue to apply to your information.

The Eden Bowls brand also operates in Brazil, and information may be shared between the two operations under appropriate safeguards.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own targeted advertising.** We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding 12 months.

## 5. Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies, to keep you signed in and your plan working, and analytics cookies (Google Analytics). You can control cookies through the “Cookie preferences” link in the site footer or through your browser settings. We honor browser Global Privacy Control signals.

## 6. How long we keep information

- **Account and dog information:** for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days, except what we must keep by law.
- **Order, billing and invoice records:** for the period required by tax and accounting law, generally 7 years.
- **Site access logs:** 12 months.
- **Customer care communications and photos you send:** 2 years after the request is closed.

## 7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: know what personal information we collect, use and share; access a copy of it; correct inaccurate information; delete it; opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do neither); limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect it); and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@edenbowls.com. To protect your account, we will send a confirmation link to the email address on file to verify that the request is yours before we act on it. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf, and we will ask for proof of that authorization. We respond within 45 days, as required by law, and will tell you if we need more time. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our response.

California residents: this section, together with sections 2 through 6, serves as our notice at collection under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), securely stored passwords, email verification codes at sign-up and restricted staff access to personal information. No system is completely secure. If a security incident creates a material risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 9. Children

The service is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child has created an account, we will close it and delete the information.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in the service or in the law. For material changes, we will notify you by email or by a notice on the site before they take effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
